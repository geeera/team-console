Paper Desk

Idea, signature moment and token sketch

Idea. A quiet, warm reading desk: the PM writes like a short letter in a serif, the interface speaks in a plain sans, and the only colour is a moss-green ink used for the next action. Nothing moves unless you act; when you do, the card is stamped and folds into a margin note that stays in the conversation as your recorded answer.

Signature moment. Ink stamp: tap Approve / Reject / Done and a stamp presses onto the card, its ring and mark draw in, then the card folds into an italic margin note (#id, time, "recorded as owner"). Reduced motion: an instant swap with a 120 ms fade.

Try: tap “Approve · close” on #13, switch to sheltrix (paused) and back (your place is kept), open Needs you, open Artifacts and search “ADR”. Mac keys after clicking in the window: B board, F artifacts, Y needs you, N message, / filter; focus a card and press its letter.
